## Review

- Your scope is the organisations assigned to you and the organisations below them.
- **Data entry** shows their values, statuses, evidence, and history.
- The **Dashboard** shows the completion rate of your organisations and who is late.

## Act

- Remind the people who have values left to fill in or to validate.
- Put a validated entry back to draft when it needs a correction. The contributor validates it again.
- After the end date of data entry, contributors have read-only access. You can still put a value back to draft.
