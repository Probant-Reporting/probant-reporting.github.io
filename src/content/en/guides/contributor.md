## Enter a value

1. Open **Data entry**. One sentence tells you what is left to do, and the end date.
2. Select **Start**. The first value to fill in opens.
3. Enter the value. The tool shows the N-1 value and the variance as you type.
4. Add the evidence if the KPI requires it: a PDF, JPG, or PNG file.
5. Select **Validate and next**. Validating confirms that your entry is complete.

## What the tool asks for

- A **comment** when the variance with N-1 is above the campaign's threshold, or when you answer N/A.
- An **evidence file** when the KPI requires one.
- If you change a validated value, it goes back to draft. Validate it again.

## On a phone, in your language

Data entry works the same way on a phone. The interface is available in seven languages: English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Dutch, and Portuguese. A short guide for each role opens from the help icon.
