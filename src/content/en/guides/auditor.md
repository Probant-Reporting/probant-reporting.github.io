## What you read

- **Data entry** of all the organisations in your scope: statuses, who entered and validated each value, comments,
  and evidence files.
- **Consistency**: the alerts, and how each one was handled.
- **History**: every change, with its date and its author.
- **Import / Export**, with the audit file, and **Visualisation**.

A closed year shows the organisation tree and the figures of the time, as they were published.

You have read-only access, so you can't change anything. The audit itself is still your job. {name} just gives you access to what you need.
