## Set up the tool

At first launch, the setup wizard guides you step by step: identity, levels of the organisation tree,
frameworks, and first campaign. Each step shows what contributors will see, and you can skip a step that has a default.

- **Frameworks**: install a template from the library (the voluntary standard VS, pay transparency, or the ESRS), compose a framework from existing KPIs, or import a file.
- **Organisations**: choose your levels and their names, and add your organisations.
- For a site that opens during the year, enter its opening date in **Open since**. The site counts in the totals from that date.
- **Accounts**: you create the accounts in the tool and give each person a role.

## Run a campaign

1. Open a campaign for a framework and a period. Set the end date of data entry.
2. Assign the KPIs to the contributors.
3. Check the **Dashboard**. It tells you if you're on track, and where to act.
4. Send reminders to the people who have the most left to do, or turn on automatic reminders.
5. Work through the **To be handled** list: variances and unexpected values.
6. Close the campaign. Its values become the N-1 of the next one. A closed year stays as it was published. If you reopen it, you choose whether to keep the figures of the time or to recalculate with today's organisation.

## Export

Export readable tables for Excel, or CSV files, from **Import / Export**. For the voluntary standard VS, you can export the values to EFRAG's official workbook, under **Official formats**.

## Connect AI assistants

The MCP server is off by default. Turn it on in **Administration › Settings › Assistants**. Each person then connects their own assistant, for example Claude, with a personal token from **My account**. An assistant has the rights of that person, never more. Readers and auditors only read. The history records every call.
