{name} is designed to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA: contrast, keyboard and labels.
This site follows the same rules.

## What's in place

- States are shown by a shape, a word and a colour, never by colour alone: ● Validated, ◐ Draft, ○ To fill in.
- Every function works with the keyboard. The focus is always visible.
- Every field has a label. Errors say what is wrong and how to fix it.
- The pages work from 320 px wide, with no horizontal scroll.
- Animations stop when your system asks for reduced motion.

## How it's checked

- Automatic checks with axe-core on every page of this site, in light and dark appearance, at 320, 360, 768 and
  1,280 px.
- Keyboard checks of the trial: open a value, validate, close with Escape, change role.
- The tool has its own browser and accessibility tests, run on every change.

## Known limits

- It hasn't been tested yet by someone who uses a screen reader every day.

## Report a problem

If something blocks you, [send me a message](/contact/). Tell me which page, and what you were trying to do.
