## Why it is free

In short: {name} started from a professional need. I had to collect figures from many sites and people, with
a tool that I could adapt when the needs changed. I built it as a personal project. A lot of teams have the same
problem, so I'm sharing it for free.

- There is no subscription or paid version. You only pay for the hosting, on your own server or with a provider.
- The source code is public, under the AGPL-3.0-or-later licence. See [Get {name}](/get/).
- It's already used for real reporting campaigns.
- You can export everything to Excel or CSV at any time. Nothing holds you back if you change tools.

The tools on the market are often expensive and too complex for simple needs. {name} can be another option.

If you try it and have a question, send me a message, I'm happy to help. I'm also interested in your feedback.

## How it is maintained

- Each new version is listed in [What's new](/whats-new/), with its notes.
- Your IT team applies an update with one command. The update backs up the database first.
- Every change is tested before release: calculations, roles, accessibility, performance.
